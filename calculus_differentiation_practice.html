Quick Reference: Standard Differentiation Rules

(i) Power & Radical Rule
Rewrite $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$, then apply:
$$\frac{d}{dx}\left(ax^n\right) = anx^{n-1}$$
(ii) Natural Logarithm
For a differentiable function $u(x)$:
$$\frac{d}{dx}\big[\ln(u)\big] = \frac{u'}{u}$$
(iii) Quotient Rule
For $f(x) = \frac{u}{v}$ where $v \neq 0$:
$$f'(x) = \frac{v u' - u v'}{v^2}$$
(iv) Chain Rule (Power)
Multiply by inner derivative $u'$:
$$\frac{d}{dx}\left[u^m\right] = m u^{m-1} \cdot u'$$
