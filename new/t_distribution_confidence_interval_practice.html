Confidence Interval of the Population Mean (t-Distribution)

Scenario #1 • Reference Example Round answers to 3 decimal places

Step-by-Step Solution

Textbook Format
n = 20
x̄ = 32.4 years
s = 5.8 years
df = 20 − 1 = 19
t0.01 two tails,19 = 2.861

99% Confidence Interval:

x̄ ± t s √n = 32.4 ± (2.861) 5.8 √20 = 32.4 ± 3.710
= (28.690 ; 36.110) years

Note on Margin of Error (E): E = t × (s / √n) = 3.71049... ≈ 3.710. Subtracting and adding 3.710 from x̄ = 32.4 yields the interval endpoints.
