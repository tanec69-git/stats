Factors Affecting Confidence Interval Width

Use the sliders to see how Sample Size and Confidence Level impact the interval.

State and explain briefly TWO (2) factors that could decrease the width of the confidence interval for the population proportion.

Increasing the sample size reduces the margin of error, which in turn narrows the confidence interval and makes the estimate of the population proportion more precise.

Choosing a lower confidence level decreases the critical value (z), which reduces the margin of error and results in a narrower confidence interval.
