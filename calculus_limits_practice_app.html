Quick Strategy Guide for Evaluating Limits

Type (i): Direct Substitution

For polynomials and composite power functions $\lim_{x \to c} (ax + b)^n$, plug $x = c$ directly into the expression since polynomials are continuous everywhere.

Type (ii): Indeterminate Form $\frac{0}{0}$

When direct substitution gives $\frac{0}{0}$, factor the numerator using the difference of squares $x^2 - c^2 = (x - c)(x + c)$, cancel the common factor, and then substitute.

Type (iii): Limits at Infinity ($x \to \infty$)

Divide every term in the numerator and denominator by the highest power of $x$ in the denominator ($x^2$). Terms like $\frac{k}{x}$ and $\frac{k}{x^2}$ approach $0$ as $x \to \infty$.
