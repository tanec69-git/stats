Core Characteristics (Slide Summary)

Dynamic Notes
  • A continuous distribution: Like the standard normal distribution, t can take any real value from −∞ to +∞, and the total area under every curve equals exactly 1.00.
  • Bell-shaped, but "flatter" and more "spread out": At df = 5, the center peak is lower (0.3796 vs 0.3989) because uncertainty in estimating the sample standard deviation s pushes more probability into the tails.
  • A family of t-distributions: All t-distributions are symmetric around a mean of 0, but their standard deviations differ according to sample size n:
    SD = √[ df / (df − 2) ] Current SD = 1.291
  • Approaches Standard Normal as n increases: At df = 5, the tails are noticeably heavier than Z. Drag the slider right to watch the orange peak rise and the tails pull inward toward the green normal curve.
